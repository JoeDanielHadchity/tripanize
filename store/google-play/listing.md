## Full description (max 4000)

Plan less. Travel more.

Tripanize turns "we should go somewhere" into an actual trip. Tell it where and when, and it drafts a day-by-day itinerary and a packing list. Invite your friends, decide together, keep track of who paid what, and settle up at the end. One app for the whole group.

DON'T KNOW WHERE TO GO YET?
Answer a few quick questions about where you're leaving from, when, and what you love. Get destination ideas with the best time to go, typical weather and budget notes, then start planning the one you like.

A DAY-BY-DAY PLAN IN MINUTES
• AI drafts your itinerary from your destination, dates and interests
• Swap a stop, move it to another day, or rebuild a single day
• Save ideas for later without putting them on a day
• Export your trip as a PDF and view it offline

PLAN IT TOGETHER
• Invite friends by link or email, as editors or viewers
• Group chat inside each trip
• Can't agree on dinner? Put it to a vote
• Optional live location sharing to find each other in the city. Off by default, and only while you choose to share

SPLIT COSTS WITHOUT THE AWKWARDNESS
• Add expenses and snap receipts
• Track spending against your budget
• See exactly who owes whom and settle up

WHAT'S ON WHILE YOU'RE THERE
Find concerts, festivals and local events along your route and dates, and keep your bookings in one place.

PACK SMART
Get a packing list built for your trip, weather and activities, and tick things off as you go.

FREE TO START
Planning a trip is free. Premium unlocks group chat, shared expenses and more for one trip, for everyone in the group, with no subscription. AI credits are available for extra AI requests.

Tripanize is not a travel agency. Suggested events and booking links are not confirmed reservations.
